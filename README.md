# Tabi Nihongo (working title)

A zero-cost, offline-first Japanese-learning PWA organised as a journey across Japan. Static site for GitHub Pages. **Work in progress – see docs/PROGRESS.md for what really exists.**

> Original practice material. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official paper of the JLPT. Results are "Practice estimate — not a JLPT score".

## Known limitations (shown to users too)

* **L-01** The target of 20 distinct mock exams per level (100 total) is NOT met in release 1.0. Released counts per level are shown in the app and below once content exists.
* **L-02** Learning audio is synthetic (device text-to-speech), not native-speaker exam audio.
* **L-03** No pronunciation scoring. Recording is optional, local, for self-comparison.
* **L-04** No handwriting recognition. Writing practice is self-assessed.
* **L-05** Content coverage is partial at every level; coverage badges show real counts.
* **L-06** Results are practice estimates, not convertible to official scaled scores.
* **L-07** iOS: non-installed Safari may lose storage after 7 days of non-use; install to Home Screen and export backups.
* **L-08** Ambience is stylised/procedural, not a recording of a real place.
* **L-09** Community research was limited; learner-experience claims are low-confidence.
* **L-10** No native-speaker review exists: no content is `human_reviewed`.
